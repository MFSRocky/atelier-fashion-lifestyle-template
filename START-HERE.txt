================================================================================
WELCOME TO ATELIER — QUICK ONBOARDING GUIDE
================================================================================

Thank you for purchasing ATELIER, an editorial fashion and lifestyle website template.

1. WHERE IS THE WEBSITE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The complete website source is inside the "template" folder.
Open "template/index.html" in your browser to immediately preview the site.

2. WHERE IS THE DOCUMENTATION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Comprehensive help documentation is in the "documentation" folder.
Open "documentation/index.html" for step-by-step guides on editing content,
colors, typography, connecting forms, and launching your site.

3. DIRECTORY OVERVIEW:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- template/       -> The deployable website (HTML, CSS, JS, assets)
- documentation/  -> Standalone offline HTML help guide
- marketplace/    -> Envato listing copy and tags
- preview/        -> High-resolution preview screenshots and covers
- README.md       -> Quick repository overview
- CHANGELOG.md    -> Version release notes
- CREDITS.txt     -> Font and photo attributions
- LICENSE.txt     -> License agreement terms

4. NEED HELP?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Refer to documentation/index.html or contact us through the author support page.
